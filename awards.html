Celebrating the Builders
of South India's Growth

South India's real estate sector is entering a defining phase. Cities such as Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi and several fast-growing urban centres are attracting unprecedented investment, adopting new technologies, raising design standards and reshaping the way people live and work. As the industry evolves, the conversation can no longer be limited to scale alone. Execution, trust, sustainability, customer experience and long-term value deserve equal attention.

The Vijay Karnataka Real Estate Summit & Awards has been created to recognise those who are raising these standards. Marking Vijay Karnataka's first-ever real estate awards for South India, the platform brings together developers, architects, designers, consultants and allied businesses whose work is changing the region's built environment. It is designed not simply to reward success, but to encourage better practices, spotlight meaningful achievements and celebrate organisations that are setting new benchmarks for the industry.

Every entry will undergo an independent evaluation by an eminent jury, ensuring that merit instead of market presence or brand size solely determines the winners. The result would be recognition of substance over perception and excellence over popularity.

Eligibility Criteria

General South India Presence

The participating organization should have its registered office or operational presence in South India and must have a minimum of 5 completed years of business operations in the region as of March 31, 2026. This applies to all categories except emerging categories.

 General Active Real Estate Participation

The participating organization must have at least one completed or ongoing real estate project in South India as of March 31, 2026, demonstrating active participation in the sector.
General Assessment Window

All projects, initiatives, or campaigns submitted for evaluation must fall within the defined assessment window for the 2026 awards cycle.

  • Completed projects must be operational on or before March 31, 2026.
  • Ongoing projects or initiatives must have commenced on or after April 1, 2024.
 General Regulatory Compliance

The participating organization must be compliant with applicable regulatory requirements, including registration under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA), wherever applicable.
Company Awards Developer of the Year
  • The organization must have a minimum of 10 years of real estate development experience in South India as of March 31, 2026.
  • The organization must have delivered at least 5 completed real estate projects, with a minimum of 2 projects delivered within the last 3 years.
 Company Awards Emerging Developer of the Year
  • The participating organization must have been incorporated within the last 10 years as of March 31, 2026.
  • The organization must have at least 2 completed projects.
Company Awards ESG & Climate Resilience Excellence
  • The participating organization must have a defined ESG framework or policy in place.
  • The organization must demonstrate implementation of sustainability or climate resilience initiatives within the defined timeframe.
 Project Awards Project Awards Segment Rules
  • The submitted project must be located in South India.
  • The project must be submitted either under Completed or Ongoing classification, and evaluation will be conducted accordingly.
Project Awards Integrated Development (Township / Mixed Use)
  • The project must have a minimum land parcel of 25 acres.
  • The project must include at least 3 distinct asset classes such as residential, commercial, or retail.
 Project Awards Industrial & Logistics Development
  • The project must be designed for logistics, warehousing, or industrial use.
  • The project must demonstrate tenant occupancy or leasing activity for completed projects.
Project Awards Social Infrastructure Development
  • The project must fall under healthcare, education, or senior living infrastructure.
  • The project must demonstrate operational functionality and end-user utilization.
 Project Awards PPP Infrastructure
  • The project must be executed under a formal public-private partnership arrangement with a government authority.
Project Awards Green Project of the Year
  • The project must have obtained or applied for a recognized green certification such as IGBC, LEED, or equivalent.
 Project Awards Smart Development of the Year
  • The project must demonstrate implementation of smart technologies, including IoT systems, automation, or digital infrastructure.
Project Awards Residential Project of the Year
  • The project must be classified as a residential development with defined housing units and amenities.
 Project Awards Luxury Residential Project of the Year
  • The project must be positioned within the premium or luxury housing segment, supported by high-value offerings and amenities.
  • The project must be located in a prime, premium, or emerging high-value area within the respective city.
  • Minimum unit price must be INR 3 crore and above.
Project Awards Affordable Housing Project of the Year
  • The project must have a maximum unit price not exceeding INR 75 lakh as of March 31, 2026.
 Project Awards Villa of the Year
  • The project must consist of independent villa units or low-density housing formats.
  • The project must have a minimum of 20 units.
Project Awards Commercial Project of the Year (Office)
  • The project must be developed as a commercial office asset.
  • The project must have a minimum built-up or leasable area of 1,00,000 sq. ft.
  • The project must demonstrate leasing activity, tenant occupancy, or income generation.
 Project Awards Retail Project of the Year
  • The project must be developed as a retail asset such as a mall, high street, or retail complex.
  • The project must demonstrate tenant mix or operational activity for the completed category.
Project Awards Redevelopment Project of the Year
  • The nominated project must involve redevelopment or transformation of an existing built asset or land parcel.
 Individual Awards Architect of the Year
  • The nominee must have a minimum of 8 years of professional experience in architecture as of March 31, 2026.
  • The nominee must have been involved in the design and execution of at least 5 completed projects, with a minimum of 2 projects completed within the last 3 years.
  • The nominee must be actively practicing in South India, with projects located within the region.
Company Awards Architectural Firm of the Year
  • The firm must have completed at least 5 projects.
  • The firm must demonstrate experience across multiple asset classes or large-scale developments.
Individual Awards Business Leader of the Year
  • The nominee must have a minimum of 10 years of experience in the real estate sector as of March 31, 2026.
  • The nominee must have held a leadership role for at least 2 years in the current organization.
  • The nominee must currently hold a leadership role such as CXO, Director, VP, or equivalent.
Individual Awards Woman Leader of the Year
  • The nominee must be a female and have a minimum of 10 years of professional experience in the real estate sector as of March 31, 2026.
  • The nominee must have held a leadership role for at least 2 years in the current organization.
  • The nominee must currently hold a leadership role such as CXO, Director, VP, or equivalent.
 Individual Awards Emerging Leader of the Year
  • The nominee must have up to 10 years of professional experience in the real estate sector as of March 31, 2026.
  • The nominee must have a minimum of 1 year of tenure in the current organization as of March 31, 2026.
Individual Awards CMO / Marketer of the Year
  • The nominee must have a minimum of 10 years of professional experience in real estate marketing.
  • The nominee must have held a leadership role for at least 2 years in the current organization.
  • The nominee must currently hold a senior marketing leadership role such as CMO, Head of Marketing, Marketing Director, or equivalent.
 Company Awards Real Estate Agency of the Year
  • The agency must have closed a minimum of 50 property transactions in the last 3 years as of March 31, 2026, across residential and/or commercial segments.
Company Awards Residential Sales Performance
  • The participating organization must have achieved a minimum of INR 100 crore and above residential sales value or volume threshold over FY 2025-26 as of March 31, 2026.
 Company Awards Boutique / Niche Developer of the Year
  • The participating organization must have at least 1 completed project within a specific niche segment as of March 31, 2026.
  • The total property portfolio size should not exceed 5 completed projects as of March 31, 2026.
Project Awards Ultra Luxury Project (Residential / Commercial)
  • The project must be positioned in the ultra-luxury segment within either residential or commercial real estate.
  • The project must be located in a prime, premium, or high-value micro-market or business district.
  • The project must demonstrate exceptional design, high-end specifications, exclusivity, and superior quality of development.
  • Residential: minimum unit price of INR 10 crore and above.
  • Commercial: presence of marquee tenants, high-value transactions, or Grade A+ asset positioning.
Technology Awards Real Estate Website of the Year
  • The website must be owned and operated by a real estate company or agency and active as of March 31, 2026.
  • The platform must demonstrate measurable user engagement such as traffic, lead generation, or conversion metrics.
Company Awards Emerging Markets Developer of the Year
  • The participating organization must have a minimum of 7 years of real estate development experience in South India as of March 31, 2026.
  • The organization must have delivered at least 3 completed real estate projects in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, with a minimum of 2 projects delivered within the last 3 years.
  • At least 70% of the organization’s project portfolio by number of projects or revenue must be concentrated in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets in South India.
 Project Awards Project of the Year
  • The project must be completed and operational on or before March 31, 2026.
  • The project may belong to any asset class such as residential, commercial, mixed-use, or retail.
  • The project must demonstrate excellence across design, execution, sustainability, innovation, and market success.
  • The project must have achieved strong market recognition, occupancy, or user adoption.
Project Awards Excellence in Design Project
  • The project must fall under either architecture or interior design within residential or commercial real estate.
  • Residential entries should show premium positioning, high-value units, or strong design-led differentiation.
  • Commercial entries should show Grade A or Grade A+ specifications, quality occupiers or tenants, or enhanced workplace and environmental experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply in more than one category?+

Yes. Applicants may apply in multiple relevant categories. However, a separate application form must be submitted for each category.

In which language should the application be submitted?+

All applications must be submitted in English.

How do I submit my nomination?+

Applicants must complete and submit the online application form available on the official awards website. All mandatory fields must be filled in before submission.

Is there an application fee?+

Yes. The standard nomination fee is 40000 INR + GST, with an early bird fee of 30000 INR + GST for entries submitted within the early bird window.

Is it mandatory to submit supporting documents?+

Certain supporting documents may be mandatory to verify eligibility and claims. Applicants are encouraged to provide relevant supporting information to strengthen their submission. Incomplete or unverifiable applications may not be considered for jury evaluation.

What happens after submission?+

Applications will first be reviewed for eligibility and completeness. Eligible entries will proceed to jury evaluation. Shortlisted nominees may be contacted for clarification, if required. Incomplete or ineligible applications will not proceed to the jury round.

Can I edit my application after submission?+

Once the application is submitted, changes cannot be made. Applicants are advised to review all information carefully before final submission.

What is the last date for submission?+

August 2026

When will the results be announced?+

Winners will be announced at the official award ceremony. The date and venue are communicated on the website and through official communication channels.

Can an award be withheld?+

The organisers reserve the right to withhold an award in any category if entries do not meet the required standards.

Who cannot participate?+

Employees of the Awards Management, jury members, sponsors, and their immediate family members are not eligible to participate. Applications not meeting the eligibility criteria may be disqualified.
