About the Summit

A credible forum for
South India's real estate future

South India's real estate market covers a lot of ground — technology-led residential demand, Grade A commercial offices, Global Capability Centres, industrial corridors, premium housing, integrated townships and a growing constellation of emerging investment destinations. It's one of India's most diversified property markets.

Bengaluru sits at the centre of this. A globally connected city powered by technology, employment, entrepreneurship and expanding infrastructure, it draws sustained demand from both domestic investors and the global Indian diaspora. At the same time, the market is asking more of itself — strong demand for professionally managed, RERA-compliant and sustainably designed projects signals a buyer and investor base that is prioritising trust, quality and long-term value. The gap between projects that meet that standard and those that don't is widening.

What this ecosystem needs is a credible forum — one that brings policy and industry into the same room, builds confidence among investors and homebuyers, recognises transparent and responsible development, and gives visibility to emerging opportunities across Karnataka and South India. The VK South Real Estate Leadership Summit & Awards 2026 aims to bridge this gap.

The Agenda

What We'll Discuss

Six defining conversations on where South India's real estate market is headed — and who needs to do what to get there.

01

Bengaluru 2035

What building a globally competitive, liveable and investment-ready city over the next decade actually requires — and who needs to do what to get there.

02

Policy, Regulation & Trust

How RERA compliance and transparent governance frameworks translate into sustained investor confidence in practice.

03

The Global Indian Opportunity

Making Bengaluru the preferred real estate destination for NRI and global Indian investors — what that takes, and what's already working.

04

GCCs, Tech & Commercial Real Estate

How Global Capability Centres are reshaping commercial demand and office space design across the city.

05

PropTech, AI & Data

How property discovery, transactions and asset management are being transformed through digital tools and data-driven approaches.

06

Capital & Confidence

The future of real estate finance in South India — REITs, institutional investment and alternative capital structures.

Invitation Only

Four Exclusive Forums

Four invitation-only forums run alongside the main programme, each providing deeper, more focused conversation within a defined thematic space.

Forum I

The Bengaluru Investment Outlook

A closed-door roundtable for senior developers and institutional investors, examining capital flows, emerging corridors and market confidence indicators for 2025–2030.

Forum II

The Global Indian Property Forum

Addressing NRI investment pathways, taxation considerations, legal compliance frameworks and long-term asset management strategies for overseas Indian investors.

Forum III

The RERA–Developer Dialogue

Bringing RERA representatives and the development community together for structured, solutions-oriented conversation.

Forum IV

Women in Real Estate Leadership

Recognising and amplifying the voices of women shaping strategy, design, policy and investment across the South India sector.
