A credible forum for South India's real estate future

South India has emerged as one of India's strongest real estate growth engines. From Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai to Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Coimbatore, Mysuru and other fast-growing cities, the region is driving the next phase of residential, commercial, industrial and mixed-use development. Backed by technology-led growth, manufacturing expansion, Global Capability Centres, infrastructure investments and rising urbanisation, South India's real estate story is shaping national growth.

At the heart of this momentum is Bengaluru – a city that continues to attract global investment, talent and enterprise while setting the pace for innovation in urban development. Its experience offers valuable lessons for the wider region, making it the ideal host for a summit that brings together leaders from across South India.

Yet, the South India real estate sector stands at an inflection point. Housing affordability must be balanced with project viability. Faster approvals, regulatory certainty and modern urban planning have become business priorities. Infrastructure must keep pace with rapid expansion, while water security, climate resilience and sustainable construction are no longer optional.

The VK South Real Estate Summit and Awards 2026 has been conceived as a forum where these conversations move beyond individual markets. More than a conference, it is a platform to align policy with industry priorities, encourage responsible and sustainable development, and strengthen confidence among homebuyers, investors and all stakeholders.

Four Exclusive Forums

Forum I

The Bengaluru Investment Outlook

A closed-door roundtable for senior developers and institutional investors, examining capital flows, emerging corridors and market confidence indicators for 2025–2030.

Forum II

The Global Indian Property Forum

Addressing NRI investment pathways, taxation considerations, legal compliance frameworks and long-term asset management strategies for overseas Indian investors.

Forum III

The RERA–Developer Dialogue

Bringing RERA representatives and the development community together for structured, solutions-oriented conversation.

Forum IV

Women in Real Estate Leadership

Recognising and amplifying the voices of women shaping strategy, design, policy and investment across the South India sector.
